Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Reach out to our team.

Can I switch plans at any time? +
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When you upgrade, you'll be prorated for the remaining billing period. When you downgrade, the new rate takes effect at the start of your next billing cycle. No penalties, no lock-in.
What happens when my free trial ends? +
Your 14-day Pro trial includes full access to all Pro features. When it ends, you can choose to subscribe or your account will automatically move to the Free plan. You won't lose any data — you just won't have access to Pro features until you upgrade.
Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or education? +
Absolutely. We offer 50% off all paid plans for registered nonprofits and educational institutions. Contact our team with your organization details and we'll get you set up.
How does billing work for added team members? +
Pricing is per workspace, not per seat. Whether you have 5 or 50 team members on the Pro plan, you pay the same monthly price. Enterprise customers with more than 50 members should contact sales for custom pricing.
