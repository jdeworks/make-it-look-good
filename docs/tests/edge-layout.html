5. Extreme line length

This is a very long paragraph with no max-width constraint. On a wide screen, the line length will exceed 75 characters significantly, making it hard for the eye to track back to the beginning of the next line. The ideal line length is 45-75 characters, with 66 being the sweet spot according to Bringhurst. This paragraph deliberately exceeds that to trigger the line length check in the typography scoring module.
