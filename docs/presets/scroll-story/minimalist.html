About

I am a designer focused on reduction. Every project starts by asking what can be removed. What remains should feel inevitable.

Based in Copenhagen. Formerly at Pentagram and Designit. Fifteen years of practice across identity, editorial, digital product, and type design. Available for select projects and long-term collaborations.

I believe the best design disappears. It does not demand attention; it earns trust through clarity, consistency, and quiet confidence.
