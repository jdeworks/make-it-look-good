Story

I fell into code the way most people fall into hobbies -- by accident, and then completely. What began as curiosity about how the web worked became a career spent making it work better.

Along the way, I discovered that I cared as much about how things looked as how they functioned. I stopped choosing between design and development and started doing both.

Now I build products end-to-end. The best work happens when one person holds the full picture -- from the database schema to the button radius.

Philosophy

Simplicity is the hardest thing to achieve and the first thing users notice.

I remove until there's nothing left to remove. Every element earns its place. The goal is software that feels quiet -- that respects attention rather than demanding it.

Good defaults over endless options. Convention over configuration. Ship what matters, say no to the rest.

Clear thinking, clean code, calm interfaces.
