My Story

It started with a curiosity that wouldn't quit. At fifteen, I was tearing apart websites to understand how they worked, rebuilding them pixel by pixel until they felt right. That obsession never faded -- it just evolved.

Over the years, I moved from tinkering to building real products. I learned that great software isn't just about clean code or beautiful interfaces -- it's about the moment a user forgets they're using a tool and just does the thing.

Today, I work across the full stack, bridging the gap between design thinking and engineering execution. I believe the best digital experiences come from people who care about both sides equally.

Philosophy

I believe software should feel inevitable -- like it couldn't have been built any other way.

That means sweating the details others skip. It means writing code that reads like prose and designing interfaces that teach themselves. It means saying no to features that dilute the vision, and yes to the craft that makes the difference.

Ship fast, iterate honestly, and never stop learning. That's the work.
